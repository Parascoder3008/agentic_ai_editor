Claude ke 4 levels hain, aur zyada tar business owners Level 1 pe hi atke hain.
Aap kaunse level pe ho? Dekhte hain.
Level 1: Chat. Sawaal poocha, jawab copy kiya. Bas.
Level 2: Projects. Apni price list, FAQs aur brand ki tone ek baar Project mein daal do.
Ab har jawab aapke business ki bhasha mein aata hai.
Level 3: Connectors. Gmail, Calendar aur Drive jodo.
Ab Claude khud emails padh ke reply draft karta hai, aur meeting calendar mein daal deta hai.
Aur bhejne se pehle aapse poochta hai.
Level 4: Scheduled tasks. Ek baar bolo: har subah 9 baje kal ki saari enquiries ki summary bhejo.
Aur ye roz apne aap hota hai, laptop band ho tab bhi.
Yahan Claude chatbot nahi, aapka assistant ban jaata hai.
Har level ka setup aur ready prompts maine ek free guide mein daal diye hain.
Chahiye to follow karo aur comment karo LEVEL. Guide seedha aapke DM mein.
