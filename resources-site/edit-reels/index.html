What this actually is

I shot one raw take on a tripod — talking, re-saying lines, asking for effects out loud. Then I handed it to Claude inside Claude Code. It cut the pauses and retakes, graded the colour, built every motion graphic, wrote the captions, mixed the sound, and checked its own work.

Honest noteIt is not one prompt and done. It took a plan, a first preview, and a few rounds of notes. That's normal — it's how you'd work with a human editor too. Section 08 lists everything that broke so you can skip it.

02 · Install everything with one prompt

Let Claude set itself up

Open Claude Code in any empty folder and paste this. It asks before each install.

Prompt · setupcopy
I'm not technical, so do everything for me and explain each step simply. I want you to edit my videos. 1. Check this computer for Node.js 22+, FFmpeg, Python 3 and Whisper (faster-whisper on Windows, whisper-cpp on Mac). Install what's missing. 2. Set up these open-source projects as skills: github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes github.com/browser-use/video-use 3. Run "npx hyperframes doctor" and fix whatever it flags. 4. Finish with a plain list of what's installed. Ask me before each install.
Before you renderKeep at least 15 GB free and close heavy apps. Video renders are hungry — on my laptop, low RAM was the first warning the tools gave me.
Mac or Windows?Both work. I edited this reel on a Windows laptop — Claude handled the Windows-specific fixes itself.

03 · Film it right

Five minutes that decide everything

  • Landscape (16:9) on a tripod. The split-screen stacks two landscape halves.
  • Light on your face, 1 metre off the wall. Clean edges = clean cut-outs.
  • Say the effect out loud, then pause. "Claude ka logo yahan lao." Pause. Claude times the effect to that exact word.
  • Hold still where an effect lands. Open palm 2 sec for the logo. Arms crossed for the documentary. Hand on head for the "hit".
  • Keep your flubs. Re-say the line. Claude keeps the clean take and cuts the rest.
  • Separate mic? Fine. I recorded on a lav and cleaned it in Audacity — Claude synced it to the camera by matching the waveforms.
  • Hinglish? Fine. Just tell Claude up front (see the trick on the next page).

04 · Give Claude eyes and ears

Everything gets timed to a word

Put your video (and separate audio, if any) in a folder, open Claude Code there, and paste:

Prompt · transcribe + lookcopy
My raw video is in this folder. [If you have one:] My clean audio was recorded separately — sync it to the camera audio first. Transcribe every word with a timestamp. I speak Hinglish: write captions in Roman script (not Devanagari), and use forced alignment so every word has an exact start and end. Then pull one frame per second and look at them. Tell me what I say, when I say it, and what's in the shot. Spell these right: [your name], [your brand], Claude, Opus.
The Hinglish trick (learned the hard way)Plain Whisper either translates Hinglish into English or gets stuck repeating words. What worked: transcribe in Hindi in short chunks, let Claude rewrite each line in Roman Hinglish, then force-align every word back to the audio. Ask for exactly that and you'll skip an hour of my pain.

05 · Plan before Claude builds anything

Changes on paper are free

Prompt · beat sheetcopy
Before you build anything, write a beat sheet as a table: start/end time, my exact words, what appears on screen, sound effect. Format: 9:16 reel. Top half = the edited video. Bottom half = my ORIGINAL take, so people see the before/after. Keep text out of the bottom 20% and away from the right edge. Brand: [your colours], [your font]. Wait for my OK before you build.

A change in the beat sheet costs one line. A change after a render costs a re-render.

06 · Every edit in my reel, with the prompt

Steal these

1Cut the dead pauses and bad takes

On camera: "…jahan pe bhi main ruka tha, bad pauses, bad takes — maine kuch nahi kiya."

Promptcopy
Cut every pause longer than 0.45 sec and every repeated or flubbed line. Never cut inside a word — snap each cut into silence. Tell me the new length. While I talk about bad takes, show an editing timeline: my real waveform, pauses grey, bad takes red. A "Claude" playhead sweeps and the counter drops from raw length to final length.

Mine: 5:09 → 1:32, 32 cuts, none audible.

2The colour grade

Promptcopy
Grade only the top (edited) half: a bit more contrast, warmer skin, slightly cooler shadows, soft vignette. Leave the bottom original untouched so the difference is obvious. Show me before/after first.

3The hook — a giant word behind you

On camera: "Bhai, maine apne video editor ki chhutti kar di."

Promptcopy
For the first 3 seconds, cut me out of the background. Put my words on a dark brand background, word by word as I say them. On "chhutti", slam CHHUTTI in huge letters BEHIND me with a thud. Make sure the word fits the frame and isn't hidden by my head.

4Logo on your palm → glass breaks

On camera: "Claude ka logo yahan lao… 3D mein spin karo… camera mein jaake hit kare, aur glass break ho jaye."

Promptcopy
On "yahan", put the Claude logo on my open palm with a few sparkles. On "spin", spin it like a thick 3D coin (fake the 3D in 2D). On "camera", fly it at the lens. On "hit", crack glass across the WHOLE screen, both halves. On "break", let the shards fall.

5The workflow demo

On camera: "Mere ko side pe le jao, aur mere teen operating systems dikhao."

Promptcopy
Shrink me into a round webcam bubble on the right, face centred. Turn the rest into a product demo timed to my words: my 3 systems (Content OS, Business OS, Money OS) light up as I name them, then show ideation, script writing, webinars and the bot dashboard. Use made-up names. Label it "illustrative demo".

6Your reels orbit you — then one hits your head

On camera: "Meri reels le ke aao… when I click one, let it hit my head." (grab your head) "Oh no, ye kya kiya?"

Promptcopy
Orbit my reel covers around my hand on a tilted oval — back cards dimmer, front cards sharp. On "click", one card breaks out, drops on my head in the top video with a boop, bounces, then falls into the ORIGINAL video and hits me again.

Tip: give Claude your Instagram handle to pull your real reel covers.

08 · What broke (so you skip it)

Every problem, and the one-line fix

The transcript came back in English, not what I said.
Whisper translates Hinglish. Fix: "Transcribe in Hindi in short chunks, rewrite in Roman Hinglish, force-align the words."
My separate audio was 21 seconds out of sync.
Fix: "Sync my audio file to the camera audio by matching the waveforms." It found the offset to the millisecond.
Effects drifted half a second late by the end.
Fix: "Make every cut a whole number of frames." Tiny rounding adds up over 30+ cuts.
The cut-out came out empty / the black-and-white step erased me.
Fix: "Check every cut-out over a solid colour before you use it." Claude found a known quirk and fixed it.

08 · What broke (continued)

Sound effects drowned my voice.
Fix: "Measure the levels. Effects no more than 3 dB above my voice."
Text hidden behind my head, cards over my face.
Fix: "Before showing me, check a frame at every effect moment over the real footage."
Renders crawling / failing.
Fix: "Render one thing at a time with one worker." Close the preview and other apps.
The one prompt that saves the most time"Before you show me anything, review your own work: frames at every effect, audio levels, every cut boundary. Fix what's below standard, then show me."
