# Equal AI ad: "Mera Personal Assistant" (script v2, 2026-10-12)

**Format:** 9:16 reel, about 38–40 s, Hinglish. **Paid partnership with Equal**, so the "Paid partnership" label goes on.
**Concept:** You're always busy (meeting, gym, late night). Every time your phone rings, an assistant in a lime-green overshirt quietly takes the call and you never break stride.
- **The twist:** the assistant *is* the app on your phone.
- After the twist, the real app takes over (the brief's rule: "show the product, let the product do the talking").

**Positioning:** "your personal AI assistant". It is never called a spam blocker or a call-screening app.
**Salary line:** "salary bhi nahi maangta" only. **No "free" or "₹0"** until the agency confirms the price; reports say paid plans are planned.
