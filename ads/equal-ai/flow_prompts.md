# Equal ad intro: Google Flow prompts (Omni Flash), v2, built for editing

## Flow settings (same for all 3)
- **Model:** Omni Flash · **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 (portrait) · **Outputs:** 2 · **Length:** 8 s (or the longest available).
- **Ingredient "Paras":** 2–3 photos of you facing the camera, glasses on, even light, no cap or sunglasses. Use the same ingredient in all 3 prompts.

## Why each prompt is written this way (so the edit is easy)
| Built into the prompt | Why it matters in the edit |
|---|---|
| Camera on a tripod, completely still | I can place a clean "incoming call" screen on the phone and add realistic handheld shake myself |
| Phone flat, screen up, in the lower third, never touched | The call screen I add stays locked to it; a moving or flipped phone can't hold an overlay |
| Phone lights up at **exactly 2 s** in every shot | Ring sounds and cuts land on the same beat in all three; the 2 s before it is lead-in |
| Reaction from 2–6 s, then a still **hold to 8 s** | Gives clean in and out points for tight cuts |
| Face stays in frame and lit, glasses on, hands away from face | Your face stays recognisable; hands touching faces are where AI warps |
| Deep focus (phone and face both sharp) | The call screen and the reaction are both clear |
| No speech, no music, ambience only | Caller voices and sound effects are added cleanly in the edit |
| No text or logos anywhere | No garbled AI text and no brand problems |

# v3: rewrites of Meeting and Gym (Paras, 2026-10-12: "the first two aren't up to the mark, the night one is good")

**Why the night shot worked:**
- One person.
- One light source you can see in the shot (the lamp).
- Close framing.
- One simple action.

**Why the meeting and gym shots probably failed:**
- **Meeting:** three people, a long table and a phone far from his face. Multi-person depth is where the model falls apart: faces, hands and perspective.
- **Gym:** a curl that stops halfway and then a rest on the knee is hard motion to get right. A plain fluorescent gym also looks flat and cheap.

**The fix:** both rewrites follow the night shot's recipe.
- **Meeting:** one blurred client shoulder at the frame edge, chest-up framing, soft window light.
- **Gym:** a treadmill run. Running is easy motion for the model, the phone on the console tray is natural, and the console light gives a visible light source.
